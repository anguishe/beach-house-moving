## House style (derived from the 13 live replies)

- **Open on their first name**, and vary the opener: "Thanks for the review Kole," /
  "Dede, that one means a bunch." / "Thank you, Rachel!" / "Tammy, ten moves –".
- **Quote or echo their own words back**, then tie them to what the crew aims for
  (on time, careful, respectful, fair, communicative).
- **Crew names only if the reviewer named them.** Never introduce names they didn't use.
- **One specific detail from the review** (the Friday heat, Grandma's things, ten moves,
  storage → apartment, junk removal). No generic "we appreciate your feedback".
- **Close with an open door** ("We're here whenever you need us", "look forward to the
  eleventh move").
- **Sign-off:** `— Beach House Moving` (newest style) or `- The Beach House Moving Team`.
- **Length:** 40–120 words, 2–4 short paragraphs.
- **Never:** prices (even if the review shows a price attribute), phone numbers, URLs,
  addresses/neighborhood of the job, promo offers, keyword stuffing ("best movers in
  Santa Rosa Beach"), details the reviewer didn't share.

## Why these pass Google's checks

Google removes owner replies that break its content policy — personal info, links/spam,
promotional or off-topic content, offensive language, impersonation. It does not remove a
reply for sounding polished. What reads as "AI/templated" to people is sameness: stock
openers, no specifics, identical wording across replies. The style above avoids all of
that; each reply is built from the reviewer's own words and never repeats another reply.
