Ranked by value for effort. All Claude work unless noted.

   into the email HTML unescaped today, so anyone can inject HTML into the owner's inbox. Small

2. **Lead source in every quote email.** Hidden fields for landing page, referrer and first-touch
   source. Set a first-party `bhm_src=gbp` flag before the `/?utm…gbp` 301, so GBP stops showing up
   as "direct/organic". The owner can see where each lead came from without logging in anywhere.
3. **`/contact` form fires no GA4 event.** Add `generate_lead` (form: contact). Also send
   `move_type` with the quote event, and add the GTM trigger for the site's own
   `phone_click_location` (hero / sticky bar / footer), which never reaches GA4 today.
4. **Quote form: add "home size / bedrooms"** (the thing that sets the price) and "How did you hear
   about us?" Add one line under the button: "We reply within X hours". The owner picks X.
5. **Lead backup.** Leads exist only as an email. If Resend hiccups, the lead is gone. Append each
   one to a Google Sheet or a small KV store.
6. **Monthly lead report, starting with the September numbers above.** One page: GBP calls /
   website clicks / views, site quotes + call taps, top queries and positions, reviews added,
   what we shipped. This is the retainer's receipt.
7. robots.txt `Disallow: /_next/` blocks Googlebot from CSS/JS. Remove it. The Ahrefs key is
   hardcoded in `layout.tsx:55`; move it to env.

## 4. Retainer path: $300 → $400 → $500

### Where it came from
June build $500 · July $500 (web + search + social) · **August: owner dropped social → $300**
(web + search) · September $300 (hosting $84, GBP $72, SEO/AEO/GEO $144; list $1,250).
**So $500 isn't a new number to them. It's what they paid when social was in.**

### The rule that makes each step explainable
**Never raise the price on the same work.** Each step adds a named line to the invoice that the
owner can point at, and it ships (or runs for a month) *before* it's billed. Keep the
"founding-client rate" framing: the list price stays struck, and the discount just shrinks.

### Step 1 → $400/mo: "Leads you can count"
New line items (~$100): **Monthly lead report** · **lead-source tracking in every quote email** ·
**pricing page build + upkeep** (the owner asked for a stronger one) · **review-request system**
(text template + QR card for the truck, target 1 new review every 10–14 days) · **2 GBP posts/week
+ same-day review replies** (already happening, now written on the invoice).

Timeline:
- **~10/1: October invoice at $300.** Add a one-line note: "Starting this month we're adding a
  monthly lead report so you can see where the calls come from."
- **October:** ship items 1–5 from §3 and the pricing page (as soon as the owner answers C1–C7).
- **~11/1: send the first lead report** (September + October, calls vs. August). The November
  invoice goes out at $300 with notice: **"$400 from December 1"**, with the new lines listed.
- **12/1: $400.** Gate: calls in the Oct report ≥ August's level and the pricing page is live.
  If calls dropped, hold at $300 one more month. The price has to follow the evidence.

### Step 2 → $500/mo: "Don't miss the calls you're paying for"
Pick the one that matches what the owner is feeling. A 4-person crew is on jobs all day, so more
calls means **more missed calls**. That's the strongest $500 story:
- **Missed-call text-back / AI receptionist** (the Saltwater offer: $150–300 setup + $50–100/mo).
  Answers after hours or while the crew is on a job, takes the move details, texts the owner.
- **Call tracking**: tracking number on the site + as GBP's primary (the real number stays as the
  additional number, so NAP is safe). Gives real, recorded call counts per channel.
- **Seasonal pages + monthly Field Note**: snowbird arrivals Oct–Nov and departures Mar–Apr, PCS
  season (Eglin/Hurlburt: that query already sits at 14.8), 30A rental turnover.
- **Google Ads / Local Services Ads management** (no Ads tag today; the $500 Google credit offer
  is sitting on their profile). Ad spend billed separately.
- Or **social comes back**. That was the original $500.

Timeline: **pitch in the February report, $500 from March 1, 2027**, ahead of the spring move
season (snowbird departures + summer peak). Gate: 3 straight reports showing calls + quotes at or
above the fall level, and a missed-call number we can point at (from the receptionist trial or the
call-tracking data).

## 5. Caveats
- The September GBP numbers run to ~9/23, not a full month. GA4 counts are small (single digits),
  so treat them as direction, not rates.
- GBP calls = taps on "Call", not connected calls. There's no call recording. Call tracking fixes that.
- The site's `/?utm…gbp` 301 deliberately hides GBP as a source in GA4 (it's what fixed the
  homepage ranking). §3.2 recovers attribution without undoing it.
