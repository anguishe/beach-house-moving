# Keyword map and ranking targets (2026-10-02)

Goal (Travis): Beach House Moving in the **Maps top 3** and on **organic page 1** for 6–8 priority searches, and on page 1
for as many more as possible.

Sources: GSC (16 months), Keyword Planner (US, Sep 2025–Aug 2026, ranges), competitor on-page scan (titles, H1/H2, nav
of 16 local competitor sites), and competitor GBP categories. Raw data is in the private research folder.

## How ranking actually works for BHM (so the targets are honest)
- **Maps / local pack:** relevance (categories, services, page content), **distance from BHM's hidden business location
  in Santa Rosa Beach**, and prominence (review count and velocity, links, citations). Distance is fixed, so the Maps
  top 3 is realistic in and around Santa Rosa Beach / 30A / Miramar / Freeport, and for specialty searches (piano, junk
  removal, organizing) where competitors are thin. In Destin, FWB and PCB, the realistic play is **organic page 1**
  through the town pages, with Maps as a stretch that depends on reviews.
- The GBP service-area list **does not boost ranking**. It sets where the profile says you serve.
- **Competitors' on-page SEO is weak.** Of 16 local sites, most titles are only the brand name ("Muscle Movers",
  "Good News Movers"). The few that target "[Town] movers" are the ones to beat on those terms.

## Keyword patterns to use in builds and edits
These are the patterns local competitors use, plus the ones they're missing:
- Titles and H1s: **"Movers in [Town], FL"** and **"[Town] Movers"** (both get searched). Lead with the town on every geo page.
- Phrases competitors rank with that BHM barely uses: **"local and long distance moving"**, **"full service moving"**,
  **"residential and commercial movers"**, **"free estimate"**, **"junk removal in [Town], FL"**, **"designer delivery"**.
- Phrases nobody local uses (open lanes): "vacation rental furniture installation", "senior / downsizing moves",
  "piano movers [Town]", "same-day movers [Town]", "is my mover licensed in Florida", "condo move rules Destin".
- Every geo and service page: an FAQ answering the autocomplete questions (in the question bank), with FAQPage schema,
  plus links to 2+ related service or town pages.
